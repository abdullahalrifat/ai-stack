# Real Jarvis efficiency acceptance criteria

For each workload class, collect enough observations to compare local-only, automatic, and cloud-first routing.

Measure success rate, incorrect completion rate, tool failures, latency, input/output/cache tokens, and estimated USD cost.

Do not change routing thresholds from a single run. Require the runtime calibration minimum sample count and quality floor, then prefer the lowest-cost route that remains above the quality floor.
